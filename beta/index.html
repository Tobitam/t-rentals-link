FAQ

Good questions

How does booking work?

Send your request here. We hold your items for 72 hours while we confirm and send bank details for a 25% deposit (minimum £20). Your booking is confirmed once the deposit lands. The rest is due on the day.

Is the deposit refundable?

No. The deposit secures your date and stops us turning other customers away. If you need to move your date, message us and we'll do our best.

Where do you deliver?

Bamber Bridge (£5 each way), Preston (£8), Blackburn (£10) and Manchester (£15). Elsewhere in the North West, just ask. Collection from Bamber Bridge is free.

What about wind and rain?

We check the forecast before every tent booking and anchor every tent before handover. If the wind is unsafe, we'll talk to you about options before the day.

Do heaters need power?

Yes. Each heater needs its own standard 13A socket nearby. Tick the power box when booking and we'll bring the right extension leads.

What if something gets damaged?

Normal wear is on us. Chairs or items that come back broken or stained beyond cleaning are charged at replacement cost. We'll always show you first.
